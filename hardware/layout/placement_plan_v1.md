## 1. Stackup: JLCPCB JLC06161H-3313 (6 layers, 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner)

| L2 | **solid GND** (reference for L1 and L3) |

| L5 | power planes: VSYS, +5V_CM5 (wide pour under CM5 5V pins), VBAT, 3V3 islands |

## 5. Power / PMIC / thermal zoning (top side)
- **Input/charger zone**, bottom-left, X -1..15, Y -33..-14: STUSB4500, Q1, BQ25895 plus its 1 µH/2.2 µH inductor (max 4.9 mm; choose ≤2.0 mm, e.g. 4×4×2). Keep the input loop ≤10 mm from USB-C. Put VBUS/VSYS/BAT pours on L1 and duplicate them on L5. Thermal vias go into L2/L4.
- **Boost zone**, X 0..14, Y -12..4: TPS61088 with ≥10 thermal vias under its PowerPad, a 1 µH/≥6 A inductor ≤2 mm tall, and output caps. +5V_CM5 runs on an L5 pour to the J2 and J1 5V pins (the CM5 5V pins are on J1 pins 77–87 area; confirm against the netlist). Load switch Q2/Q3 sits near the boost output. Keep the boost hot loop on L1 only, over solid L2.
- **MCU + AON LDO**, X 2..12, Y 8..20 (near J_BATT, buttons flex via L3), plus the case-NTC connector.
- **Audio** (MAX98357A) near the flex at X 36..44, Y -33..-28. Its BCLK/LRCLK/DOUT come from J2-side GPIOs.
- **Sensors** (VEML7700 sits behind the ALS window at (-58,20) on the far left, so it goes on the flex, not the carrier; DRV2605L DNP) near the flex.
- Thermal: the SoC heat path is CM5 → pad → Al pedestal → back plate. The carrier top under the display holds the charger/boost: keep them ≥15 mm apart, keep the boost and charger out from under the display-backlight centre where possible, and put the charge/boost thermal vias into the L2/L4 GND. Fit a 0402 NTC on the board near the charger for skin-temperature control (BQ TS is used for the cell NTC).

## 6. High-speed routing
All MIPI and USB2 signals live on **J2** (CM5 pins 101–200, Y -27) on the CM4/CM5 pinout: USB2 D± ~103/105, MIPI0 CSI ~115–145, MIPI1 DSI ~157–193. Confirm the exact pins with the final netlist.
- **USB2 (90 Ω)**: J2 → L6 stub → via → L1 → USB-C. Length <10 mm, no stubs, ESD placed in-line on L1.
- **DSI (2 lanes + clk, 100 Ω)**: J2 B-side escape (≤3 mm) → via → **L3** stripline north under the CM5 area at X 12..20 → via near J3 → L1 into the FPC. Length ~55 mm. Intra-pair ≤0.1 mm, lane-to-clock ≤1 mm, pair-to-pair gap ≥0.45 mm (3W), 2 vias per signal max with GND stitch vias.
- **CSI (2 lanes + clk, 100 Ω)**: J2 → L3 north at X 28..36 → J4 (X 28..40, Y 7..10). Length ~38 mm. Avoid the J1 pad field: cross Y 5.6..8.4 only on L3.
- Keep L3 HS pairs away from the boost switch node (≥5 mm laterally, and L4 GND is unbroken beneath).
- I2C/I2S/GPIO go on L3/L1 in the remaining space. 5V and GND pins of J1/J2 get direct vias to L5/L2/L4.
